## Overview

TINA AUDIO (知名オーディオ) is a small, family-operated Japanese audio manufacturer based in Naha, Okinawa, founded in 1975 by Hiroshi China. Operating from a single workshop and showroom, the company designs and builds its products entirely in-house, selling cylindrical, vertically-oriented full-range speaker-and-amplifier systems directly to consumers with multi-month build times. The current four-model lineup (1.0, 1.5, 2.0, 2.0 HIGH CLASS) shares a common patented column-resonance bass technology first developed in 2005 and patented in 2020. Listed prices range from 385,000円 to 1,430,000円 per set.

## Scientific Validity

$$ \Large \text{0.5} $$

Across TINA AUDIO's four-model lineup, the only audio-quality-relevant specification the manufacturer publishes for any model is a frequency response bandwidth claim: 35Hz-20,000Hz (1.0), 30Hz-20,000Hz (1.5), 20Hz-20,000Hz (2.0), and 20Hz-20,500Hz (2.0 HIGH CLASS), each stated without a ± dB tolerance [1][2]. Because these figures describe bandwidth extent rather than in-band flatness, they cannot be directly compared against dB-deviation thresholds and are treated only as non-gradable indicators. No independent measurement organization has published any measurement of any TINA AUDIO product, and the manufacturer itself publishes no quantified THD, S/N ratio, dynamic range, SINAD, IMD, or crosstalk figures for either the passive drivers or the bundled analog amplifiers [1][2]. The company's statement that its welded internal wiring is "perceptually undetectable" with "no soldering-related distortion" is an unquantified claim rather than a measured value and is excluded from scoring. All four current models share this identical data profile — a manufacturer-only bandwidth claim with no dB tolerance and no quantified distortion or noise figures. Each model is therefore scored at the same indeterminate midpoint between problematic and transparent levels; the flagship 2.0 HIGH CLASS is weighted most heavily as the company's best capability, with the remaining three weighted equally given their shared architecture and identical data availability, yielding an overall indeterminate score for the lineup.

## Technology Level

$$ \Large \text{0.6} $$

TINA AUDIO holds a patented core technology — the column-resonance ("1/8-wavelength") bass-reproduction design, granted August 2020 — and designs and builds its products entirely in-house at a single Okinawa workshop operating since 1975 [1][3]. Decades of hands-on craftsmanship in this narrow niche support a know-how accumulation credit, and the granted patent provides a multi-year barrier against replication. However, the core technology has not advanced since its 2005 origin and 2020 patent grant: the four current models (1.0/1.5/2.0/2.0 HIGH CLASS) are scale variations — differing only in cabinet height/diameter and amplifier wattage — of the same design, with no newer technology introduced [1][2]. Since the technology is not cutting-edge by current standards, it also cannot be credited as currently desirable to competitors, and the resulting stagnant development is penalized. The product line is entirely analog and mechanical, with no DSP, computer, or digital integration [1][2]. The marketed "weld instead of solder" wiring technique is presented as producing "perceptually undetectable" distortion but has no independent or numeric verification, fitting the profile of a marketing claim rather than a demonstrated technical advance [1].

The official product page lists weight as "重量（1本）" (weight per 1 unit) [2] and bundles each model with a 2-channel amplifier meaningful only for driving a left/right pair, indicating the listed prices most likely cover a single speaker column rather than a complete stereo pair. ## Reliability & Support

$$ \Large \text{0.5} $$

TINA AUDIO offers a standard 1-year warranty with no extended options identified, below the typical multi-year threshold [1]. The speaker-and-amplifier design is structurally simple — a single full-range driver with no crossover network, paired with a basic 2-channel analog amplifier — which is inherently more resistant to component-level failure than more complex multi-way or DSP-laden designs. Support is manufacturer-direct but limited to a single physical showroom in Okinawa with restricted hours (Friday-Monday, 13:00-18:00), with no evidence of support outside Japan [1]. Repairs after the 1-year warranty are available on a paid basis with a standard estimate-and-approval process and a documented 2-3 week turnaround; no evidence of unreasonably high repair costs was found [1]. Firmware and software update considerations do not apply, as the product line is fully analog. No statistical failure-rate, RMA, or MTBF data exists for the brand, and no recalls or systematic defect reports were found.

## Rationality of Design Philosophy

$$ \Large \text{0.1} $$

TINA AUDIO's stated philosophy — "bring sound closer to live performance and deliver emotional impact," pursued through "passion, technology, and wisdom" [1] — is explicitly subjective and emotional rather than measurement-based, and the company publishes no S/N ratio, numeric THD, dynamic range, SINAD, IMD, or crosstalk data for any product. Its core technical claims — patented bass extension via column resonance, "perceptually undetectable" distortion from welded rather than soldered wiring, and 360-degree dispersion via a deflector plate — are presented as unverified manufacturer and patent assertions with no independent measurement [1][2][3]. This approach relies on subjectivity and unverified claims rather than a complete elimination of subjective marketing. The welded-wiring framing implies that ordinary solder joints introduce audible distortion that welding avoids, an audibility claim for a construction detail with no supporting THD measurement. Cost allocation is not itemized by the company, and available information points to labor-intensive handcrafted construction of tall cylindrical enclosures rather than disclosed high-specification components, so costs cannot be shown to directly track function or measured performance. The four-model lineup has shown no functional or measurable improvement since the core design was established in 2005 and patented in 2020 — differences are limited to cabinet height/diameter and amplifier wattage, representing stagnation rather than genuine progression.

## Advice

TINA AUDIO's products are best considered by buyers specifically drawn to handcrafted, single-source Okinawan manufacturing rather than by measured performance or value. No independent measurement data exists for any model, and the manufacturer publishes only frequency response bandwidth claims with no distortion or noise figures. At current pair-adjusted pricing, self-powered alternatives with broader connectivity and equal-or-better manufacturer-stated specifications are available at a fraction of the cost. The 1-year warranty and single-showroom support model in Okinawa should factor into purchase decisions, particularly for buyers outside Japan.
