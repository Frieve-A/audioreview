## Overview

Earaku is a lifestyle-audio brand operated by JUST MATCH Corporation. The official corporate profile lists a January 2013 establishment, a Tokyo head office, a China office in Shenzhen, and the manufacture and sale of earphones and voice/audio devices [1]. Its brand and laboratory pages describe a people-centered, "stress-free" concept and product R&D, design, and marketing performed with engineers worldwide, plus repeated factory testing and inspection [2][3]. The reviewed current products span ear-cuff true wireless earphones, wired open-ear earphones, ANC headphones, and a hand-held TV speaker [4][5][6][7][8]. The official material does not establish that all manufacturing is outsourced to Shenzhen partner factories, so no such manufacturing arrangement is assumed here.

## Scientific Validity

$$ \Large \text{0.5} $$

No independent frequency-response, distortion, isolation, or noise-cancelling measurements were identified for the five representative current products reviewed. F308's official material claims 0.01% distortion and 105 dBA signal-to-noise ratio and shows a response comparison, but omits signal level, analyzer, bandwidth, sample information, and the identity of the compared earphone [4]. J131 claims 22±3dB ANC reduction without a frequency range or reproducible test conditions, and its DSP/ANC diagrams also lack sufficient axes and conditions [7]. J235 shows a frequency-like diagram without sufficient axis units, device identification, or test conditions [6]. F301 and J088 publish functional specifications but no product-level fidelity measurements [5][8]. The limited manufacturer material is therefore insufficient to assess the current lineup's Scientific Validity, which remains 0.5.

## Technology Level

$$ \Large \text{0.4} $$

Earaku's laboratory page documents product R&D, design, marketing, cooperation with engineers worldwide, and factory testing, which supports design ownership at the brand level [3]. Its current products integrate established Bluetooth audio, DSP, conventional transducers, and use-specific enclosures [4][5][6][7][8]. F308's AELC function is described as real-time detection of input level and output sound pressure followed by band-balance adjustment, but the cited material does not identify it as an earaku patent or disclose its implementation [4]. No in-house patent technology, technical paper, or software platform creating a durable replication barrier was identified. Because the implementations use mature techniques and are readily reproducible despite competent product integration, Technology Level is 0.4.

## Cost-Performance

$$ \Large \text{1.0} $$

Five current products were given equal 20% weight because they represent distinct current uses and no audited sales mix is public. Japanese market prices were converted to USD with the ECB reference cross-rate; short, explicitly dated promotions were excluded in favor of regular prices [11][12][13][14][15][16].

- SoundClip F308: 96.67 USD. No cheaper product was identified with its AELC function and at least 12-hour earbud/48-hour case battery claims; F301 is cheaper but lacks AELC and claims 10 hours per charge [4][5][11][12]. CP = 1.0.
- AirCuff F301: 41.43 USD. The cheaper TRUEFREE Clip C10 at 28.85 USD lacks F301's charging-case touch display, direct five-mode EQ control, and 10-hour earbud claim, so it is not functionally equivalent [5][12][19]. No cheaper equivalent was identified; CP = 1.0.
- J235: 22.81 USD. Avantree Resolve costs 18.49 USD and provides the same relevant catalog functions: 3.5mm wired open-ear hooks, microphone, and inline volume, playback, track, and call controls [6][17][18]. Neither product has interpretable measured fidelity data, so this is a provisional catalog-function comparison. CP = 18.49 USD ÷ 22.81 USD = 0.8106.
- Soundquick J131: 33.45 USD. No cheaper product was identified with its six-axis dynamic head tracking together with ANC, two-device multipoint, microphone, and AAC. For context, the current 1MORE SonoFlow provides ANC and multipoint but lacks dynamic head tracking, so it is not equivalent [7][14][20]. CP = 1.0.
- Youon Pro J088: 113.02 USD. No cheaper finished product or accessory bundle was identified that combines the 20W speaker claim, sub-25ms 2.4GHz TV transmitter with optical and analog inputs, Bluetooth 5.3, three EQ modes, 19 sleep sounds, 15-hour battery claim, and large physical controls [8][15]. CP = 1.0.

The equal-weight average is (1.0 + 1.0 + 0.8106 + 1.0 + 1.0) ÷ 5 = 0.9621, which rounds to 1.0.

## Reliability & Support

$$ \Large \text{0.4} $$

Earaku's documented warranty is 1 year, including on the official J131 warranty certificate, and no extended-warranty program was identified [7][9]. Support is manufacturer-direct through JUST MATCH Corporation's web channels and FAQ, but the documented infrastructure is Japan-focused [1][10]. Post-warranty parts-supply duration, repair pricing, statistical failure rate, RMA rate, and MTBF are undisclosed. Documentation quality is also uneven: official J131 pages give conflicting battery-life figures [7][14], while J088 pages disagree on the speaker/transmitter dimensions and whether sleep playback stops after 30 or 45 minutes [8][15]. The short warranty is the scored negative factor, placing Reliability & Support at 0.4; the undisclosed statistics are treated as unknown rather than as additional deductions.

## Rationality of Design Philosophy

$$ \Large \text{0.4} $$

Earaku organizes its products around concrete uses such as open-ear listening, hands-free communication, and bringing television sound closer to the listener [4][5][6][7][8]. These form-factor, DSP, control, and connectivity choices provide real user functions, and no occult or physically implausible audio claim was identified. The laboratory page describes repeated testing and inspection [3], and F308 provides limited numeric performance claims [4]. However, prominent sound-quality and noise-control diagrams omit the conditions needed for verification, while the design narrative remains led by comfort and subjective listening language [2][4][6][7]. The direction is functional but not consistently measurement-led, supporting a Rationality score of 0.4 independently of whether third-party data happens to be public.

## Advice

Buyers should choose among earaku products by required wearing style, connectivity, controls, battery operation, and speaker placement rather than assuming that DSP labels establish higher fidelity. F308 has limited manufacturer figures, but their test conditions are not disclosed and no independent verification was identified; the other reviewed products provide still less interpretable performance evidence. The documented warranty is one year, and J131 battery-life figures and J088 dimensions and sleep-timer descriptions differ between official pages. Confirm those details with the seller before purchase.
