## Overview

TINA AUDIO 1.0 is a cylindrical, single-full-range-driver speaker paired with a dedicated "Amp 10" amplifier, hand-built by Okinawa-based manufacturer TINA AUDIO (知名オーディオ), founded in 1975 by Hiroshi China [1][3]. It is the entry-level model in a four-model lineup, priced at 385,000円 (2,461.48 USD) tax-included and sold via direct order [1]. The design traces to a welded-connection technique the company states was patented in 1987 and a cylindrical bass-loading concept conceived in 2002 and commercialized in 2005 [3].

## Scientific Validity

$$ \Large \text{0.5} $$

The manufacturer states a frequency response of 35Hz-20kHz for TINA AUDIO 1.0, with no ± dB tolerance or measurement method disclosed [1]. This range would be favorable if independently verified, but no third-party measurement of this speaker was found despite an exhaustive search, and without a stated tolerance the actual flatness cannot be confirmed. As a passive speaker paired with its bundled amplifier, no THD, S/N ratio, or comparable figures are published by the manufacturer. Given the unverifiable, single-source nature of the only available data point, measured performance is assessed at a moderate midpoint level.

## Technology Level

$$ \Large \text{0.2} $$

TINA AUDIO 1.0 is an in-house-designed, hand-built product with claimed patents covering its cylindrical bass-loading enclosure and its welded internal connections [1][3]. Both underlying principles — resonant-pipe/transmission-line bass extension and solder-free same-metal joining — are long-established techniques predating this product line rather than recent developments, and neither represents technology other manufacturers would need to license. No independent measurement corroborates a performance benefit from any of the three claimed technologies. The design is purely analog and mechanical with no digital signal processing, and no measurable technical advancement is documented across the 1.0/2.0/2.0 HIGH CLASS lineup. These factors outweigh the credit given for in-house design and claimed patents, yielding a below-average score.

## Cost-Performance

$$ \Large \text{0.1} $$

TINA AUDIO 1.0 currently sells for 2,461.48 USD (385,000円 tax-included) as a complete speaker-and-amplifier set, direct from the manufacturer [1]. The cheapest identified product with equivalent-or-better functions and measured performance is the PreSonus Eris E8 XT (pair), 319.98 USD at current official manufacturer-store price [4]. It is equipped with a built-in amplification system delivering substantially higher output than TINA AUDIO 1.0's bundled 10W×2 amplifier, plus balanced XLR, balanced TRS, and unbalanced RCA inputs. Its manufacturer-stated frequency response matches TINA AUDIO 1.0's manufacturer-stated range, and this low-frequency extension is corroborated by a third-party measurement-based conclusion:

## Reliability & Support

$$ \Large \text{0.3} $$

TINA AUDIO 1.0 carries a 1-year warranty, below the common 2-year baseline [1]. The speaker-and-amplifier design is structurally simple, with a single full-range driver and no crossover or DSP, which would ordinarily favor reliability; however, the manufacturer's own care documentation describes specific failure modes — condensation-related malfunction without an acclimation period after cold/humid storage, and connector or plug corrosion causing intermittent noise or dropouts that requires periodic plug rotation to mitigate — indicating a degree of susceptibility to environmental degradation [2]. Support is manufacturer-direct from a single Okinawa showroom, with no evidence of support infrastructure outside Japan and no statistical failure-rate or reliability track-record data available.

## Rationality of Design Philosophy

$$ \Large \text{0.1} $$

TINA AUDIO's stated design philosophy is explicitly experiential — "bringing sound close to live performance," conveying "the breath of vocalists" — built on the founder's decades of personal experimentation rather than a measurement-first or blind-test-based development process [1][3]. None of the product's three core technical claims — the cylindrical bass-loading enclosure, welded internal connections, and omnidirectional dispersion — has been independently measured to confirm an audible benefit. A substantial share of the price appears allocated to aesthetic presentation (the columnar form, a design award, lifestyle-magazine coverage) and founder narrative rather than demonstrated function, which itself remains unconfirmed. No measured performance difference is documented between the 1.0 and 2.0/2.0 HIGH CLASS lineup variants. Production relies on manual, small-batch handcrafting rather than a measurement-optimized approach, a choice made without stated scientific justification. The product does provide genuine functional audio output as a working speaker and amplifier set, distinguishing it from equipment with no scientifically expected audible effect whatsoever.

## Advice

Prospective buyers should treat TINA AUDIO 1.0 as an unverified, single-source product: no independent measurement confirms its manufacturer-stated frequency response or any distortion/noise performance, and equivalent-or-better measured performance is available in the PreSonus Eris E8 XT at roughly one-eighth the price. The 1-year warranty and single-showroom, Japan-only support add ownership risk. Buyers primarily motivated by the brand's handcrafted narrative and aesthetic presentation, rather than measurable audio performance, are the intended audience; those prioritizing verified fidelity per dollar should look elsewhere.
