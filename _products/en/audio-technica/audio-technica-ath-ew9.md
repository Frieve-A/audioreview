## Overview

The Audio-Technica ATH-EW9 is a wired clip-on (ear-hook) headphone from Audio-Technica's W Series, featuring a housing machined from solid Hokkaido Asada cherry wood. Released in Japan in November 2003 and introduced to the US as an Import Series product in December 2005, the ATH-EW9 remained in production for over 20 years before being discontinued with no designated successor. As of July 2026, new units are available only through Japanese import channels at 152.46 USD [5].

## Scientific Validity

$$ \Large \text{0.4} $$

The manufacturer specifies frequency response as 14–24,000 Hz with no stated deviation tolerance [1]. A third-party frequency response graph measured on a GRAS 43AG-7 / IEC 60318-7 compliant rig is available [2]; however, no numeric deviation from the Harman target curve is published and no written measurement-site conclusion accompanies the graph, making quantitative frequency response assessment from available data impossible. THD is neither specified by the manufacturer nor measured by any third-party source, leaving distortion performance entirely uncharacterized.

Passive isolation is negligible by structural design. The ear-hook clip-on attachment makes no contact with the ear canal and forms no seal around the outer ear, while the semi-open acoustic system permits bidirectional sound leakage. The resulting isolation is effectively zero, representing a confirmed acoustic weakness.

With passive isolation confirmed as minimal and the remaining primary metrics (frequency response deviation, THD) yielding no usable numeric values from any available source, the score reflects these combined data limitations and confirmed weakness.

## Technology Level

$$ \Large \text{0.3} $$

The ATH-EW9 is a confirmed in-house Audio-Technica design, and the W Series product family demonstrates internal manufacturing experience in precision solid-wood housing machining. These are the product's documented implementation positives. The 28mm neodymium dynamic driver and passive wired analog signal path are fully mature and ubiquitous technologies with no advancement to offer in 2026. No proprietary patent technology was identified for any component. The product is entirely analog and mechanical with no digital, software, or signal-processing integration of any kind. The wood housing's acoustic properties are asserted by the manufacturer without independent measurement corroboration, placing this claim outside verifiable engineering. The EW clip-on product line produced no discernible technical advancement over its 20+ year production run before discontinuation, and the overall configuration offers no competitive differentiation.

## Cost-Performance

$$ \Large \text{0.2} $$

The ATH-EW9 provides wired stereo audio via 3.5mm TRS, passive ear-hook clip-on wearing, and no active features (no ANC, no wireless, no DSP/EQ, no microphone). It includes a 0.6m cable and 1.0m extension cord.

The Koss KSC75 (24.99 USD at retail [3]) provides all equivalent user-facing functions: wired 3.5mm stereo audio and passive ear-clip wearing with no active features. Available performance data for the comparison:

- **THD**: KSC75 — 0.219% @ 90 dB SPL (RTINGS weighted THD [4]); ATH-EW9 — no data from any source. The KSC75 has measured distortion data, while ATH-EW9's absence of corresponding data does not constitute a disqualification basis for the KSC75.
- **Frequency Response**: Both products have FR graphs measured on GRAS 43AG-7 / IEC 60318-7 rigs by Crinacle [2]; neither product has a published numeric deviation from the Harman target curve. No basis exists to declare the KSC75's frequency response worse than the ATH-EW9's. Comparison is provisional pending independent numeric FR measurements for both products.
- **Sound Isolation**: Both are open/semi-open supra-aural clip-on designs with no ear-canal contact, and no documented isolation advantage is available for ATH-EW9. Equivalent.

The KSC75 additionally includes a Limited Lifetime Warranty, placing it as equivalent-or-better overall.

CP = 24.99 USD / 152.46 USD = 0.1639

Rounded to one decimal place, this supports the 0.2 cost-performance score.

## Reliability & Support

$$ \Large \text{0.6} $$

Audio-Technica maintains a global manufacturer support network spanning the Americas, Europe, and Asia-Pacific with authorized service centers. The ATH-EW9's 20+ year production history includes no documented recalls, service bulletins, or reports of widespread driver, structural, or connection failures, supporting a positive reliability assessment. The US market warranty is 2 years, which is standard. The product is now discontinued, and the Japan-market 6-year post-manufacture spare parts retention rule makes long-term parts-based repair time-limited rather than open-ended.

The passive electrical design is inherently simple, but the solid wood housing requires careful handling — documented as sensitive to moisture, heat, and direct sunlight — and the sliding clip mechanism incorporates rubber components susceptible to age-related degradation. No firmware considerations apply to this passive device.

## Rationality of Design Philosophy

$$ \Large \text{0.1} $$

The ATH-EW9's design prioritizes natural material presentation and craft-oriented manufacturing over measurable audio performance. At the current 152.46 USD import listing price, the cost premium over the KSC75 comparator is tied to the solid Hokkaido Asada cherry wood housing, one-piece machining, engraved connector, and provenance branding rather than audio-performance-differentiated components [3][5]. The manufacturer made no claims referencing measured fidelity metrics; the design narrative centered on material origins and manufacturing heritage. Wood housing acoustic benefits are claimed without any independent measurement corroboration. The product cost structure is dominated by materials and aesthetics unrelated to audio performance. No DSP, digital integration, or software was incorporated at any stage of the product's life. The product line produced no measurable performance advancement over 20+ years and concluded without a successor.

## Advice

The ATH-EW9 is currently available only as new old stock through Japanese import channels at 152.46 USD, with discontinued-model parts support. For listeners who need a functional wired clip-on headphone, the Koss KSC75 provides equivalent user-facing functionality at 24.99 USD with confirmed THD measurements from RTINGS and a Limited Lifetime Warranty, or 16.4% of the ATH-EW9's import price. The ATH-EW9's premium reflects its solid cherry wood housing and Japanese craftsmanship aesthetic, with no independently measured audio performance advantage to justify it. Collection or display use centered on the wood housing aesthetic or Audio-Technica W Series history should be treated as a non-performance reason; those evaluating on audio performance alone should direct their budget to alternatives with established measured performance data.
