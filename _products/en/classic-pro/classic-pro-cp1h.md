## Overview

CLASSIC PRO CP1H is a single-listener stereo OTL (Output Transformer-Less) headphone amplifier released January 20, 2026 under CLASSIC PRO, the in-house brand of Japan's Sound House [1]. Its regular Japanese-market price converts to 245.38 USD at the cited exchange rate [1][5], and it supports 15–600-ohm headphones. A front-panel switch selects among a balanced XLR/TRS or unbalanced TS combo input, an RCA input, and a 3.5 mm stereo input; a second switch selects the 6.35 mm or 3.5 mm headphone jack. This is the first headphone amplifier released under the CLASSIC PRO brand.

## Scientific Validity

$$ \Large \text{0.8} $$

The manufacturer specifies 0.0008% THD+N at 10 mW into 470–600 ohms through the XLR input (1 kHz, 20 kHz measurement bandwidth), a 20 Hz–20 kHz frequency response within ±0.2 dB, and 113.5 dB dynamic range at 220 ohms through the XLR input [1]. All three published metrics indicate strong performance, and the unspecified-load maximum output is 300 mW. However, the product page does not provide numerical S/N ratio, crosstalk, or output-impedance data, and no independent third-party bench measurement of this model was found. The score therefore treats the manufacturer-only data conservatively rather than assuming production units necessarily reproduce every claim.

## Technology Level

$$ \Large \text{0.2} $$

The CP1H is a private-label product manufactured for Sound House, and the cited product materials identify no patent, in-house engineering credit, or unique circuit design [1]. Its solid-state OTL topology uses decades-mature analog amplifier principles; the "OTL" label does not by itself describe a novel solid-state achievement. Wide impedance support and three-input switching are useful implementations rather than new technical principles, and the product adds no digital or software-controlled integration. The design is therefore technically competent but based on mature, readily reproducible technology, independent of its claimed measured performance.

## Cost-Performance

$$ \Large \text{1.0} $$

The CP1H's listed regular price converts to 245.38 USD [1][5]. A limited offer displayed on the review date is temporary and is not used as its representative price. The 129 USD JDS Labs Atom Amp 2 is the closest cheaper candidate found [2]. Audio Science Review independently measured 0.000096-0.000097% THD+N (120.24-120.34 dB SINAD) at approximately 1.96 V output into 600 ohms through the RCA input in low gain (1 kHz, <10 Hz-22.4 kHz bandwidth), with 124.0 dB S/N ratio [3]. These results establish excellent linearity but do not close the functional gaps.

The Atom Amp 2 has only two switchable inputs (RCA and 3.5 mm) and 6.35 mm/4.4 mm headphone jacks. It therefore lacks both the CP1H's third independently switchable source and its balanced line-input path. The CP1H's 300 mW maximum also lacks a published load condition, preventing a like-for-like maximum-output comparison. No lower-priced finished product or documented compatible selector/converter bundle was identified that preserves all three input paths, both selectable headphone connector formats, and equivalent-or-better documented performance. The CP1H is therefore the cheapest identified complete option, so its cost-performance score is 1.0.

## Reliability & Support

$$ \Large \text{0.6} $$

Sound House's general terms provide 3 years of warranty coverage from purchase, subject to listed exclusions and any manufacturer-specified period, and limit coverage to use within Japan [4]. This is longer than the common 2-year term, but it is not a global support arrangement. The conventional solid-state analog construction has no unusual failure-prone mechanism, while neither the cited materials nor statistical failure-rate data establish above-average durability; construction and field reliability therefore remain neutral. Firmware maintenance does not apply because the CP1H has no digital control or DSP.

## Rationality of Design Philosophy

$$ \Large \text{0.6} $$

CLASSIC PRO presents the CP1H as a function-first design, and its cost is directed toward three selectable input paths, two selectable headphone connector formats, and 15–600-ohm compatibility rather than decorative features [1]. No less expensive option with the complete documented function and performance set was identified, supporting the cost-focused direction. However, the product page also links selected parts and the OTL implementation to subjective claims such as natural, information-rich sound without controlled evidence that those choices are audibly superior. OTL operation is not itself a fidelity advantage, and there is no predecessor against which to establish continued performance progression. The useful functional focus outweighs the unsupported audibility language, but the design philosophy is evolutionary rather than strongly innovative.

## Advice

The CP1H is a reasonable option for users needing a compact analog headphone amplifier with three genuinely switchable source inputs, selectable 6.35 mm/3.5 mm outputs, and 15–600-ohm compatibility. Buyers prioritizing independently verified performance should note that the CP1H's figures remain manufacturer-only. The less expensive Atom Amp 2 has excellent independent bench results, but only two source inputs and no native 3.5 mm headphone jack, so it is an alternative only when those CP1H functions are unnecessary [2][3]. Buyers outside Japan should also weigh the lack of a documented global service network.
