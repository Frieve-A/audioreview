## Overview

FitEar ROOM2 is a custom-molded in-ear monitor (CIEM) from Suyama Dental Laboratory, introduced in 2025. It extends the ROOM series' aim of making custom-fit listening more accessible; the manufacturer currently lists ROOM and ROOM2 together [2][3]. Its shell is made from individual ear impressions, and the package includes the detachable FitEar cable 012 with a 3.5mm stereo plug, a semi-hard case, a cable clip, and a cleaning brush [1][2]. It provides wired playback without built-in EQ, wireless connectivity, or ANC. The current Japanese tax-inclusive product price converts to 503.60 USD; this is a currency conversion, not a verified US retail offer [3][5]. It is made to order, with impression-taking charged separately when existing ear data cannot be reused [6].

## Technology Level

$$ \Large \text{0.5} $$

The ROOM series uses in-house shell design, computer modeling of internal layouts, and 3D printing. FitEar describes accumulated expertise in accommodating ear geometry and jaw movement, and a middle-leg shell intended to balance fit and isolation [2]. These are identifiable engineering contributions, distinct from undisclosed driver details. However, passive acoustic reproduction and the documented manufacturing methods are mature technologies, and ROOM2 adds no digital signal-processing or software functionality during playback. The available documentation establishes neither an adopted model-specific patent nor a technical barrier that demonstrably requires competitors several years to reproduce. The result is a competent custom-manufacturing implementation rather than evidence of leading-edge electronic or computational integration.

## Reliability & Support

$$ \Large \text{0.5} $$

The official ROOM2 page specifies a 12-month warranty covering the earpieces; cable faults are excluded except for initial defects. Service is handled through the authorized seller [1]. Fujiya Avic separately requires fit problems to be reported within ten days of receipt for free refitting; this is distinct from the one-year product warranty [6]. The compact, battery-free passive construction and replaceable cable limit some maintenance risks, but do not establish a low failure rate. No ROOM2 failure-rate statistics, guaranteed parts-support duration, or standard repair-price schedule were identified. The short warranty and straightforward construction support a middle-of-the-road assessment, without assuming unusually good longevity or repair service.

## Rationality of Design Philosophy

$$ \Large \text{0.8} $$

The core design addresses practical needs: a repeatable personalized fit, reduced external noise, and compact wired playback. Spending on ear-specific geometry directly serves those functions. The ROOM series also uses computer-designed internal layouts and 3D printing to reduce manual work, while simplifying assembly and component selection to control manufacturing cost [2]. Digital manufacturing therefore supports a useful physical function and production efficiency. The middle-leg shell addresses the trade-off between insertion depth and fit rather than adding decorative complexity. Subjective descriptions of the sound do not demonstrate progress over ROOM, but the design's underlying emphasis on fit and efficient fabrication is rational. No unsupported claim that a non-audible accessory or material improves sound is needed to justify the product.

## Advice

ROOM2 is relevant when individualized fit and passive isolation are priorities, but its published information does not establish reproduction accuracy. The manufacturer states that made-to-order products cannot be returned or exchanged for a change of mind, and gives a typical manufacturing time of one to two months after receiving ear impressions [1]. Check fitting-adjustment terms, cable coverage, and the complete order cost before purchasing. Buyers who prioritize verifiable fidelity should seek response and distortion measurements rather than assuming that custom fit or a higher price guarantees better sound. Overseas buyers should verify an authorized local offer: FitEar expressly declines Japanese orders intended for overseas resale or forwarding, so the converted Japanese price is not an import-order quote [1].
