## Overview

The Volumio Primo Plus is a network streaming DAC and preamplifier from Volumio SRL, a Florence, Italy-based audio software company founded in 2013. Priced at 1,399 USD, the unit integrates dual-mono DAC implementation, balanced XLR and unbalanced RCA line outputs with eight selectable voltage levels, and digital inputs including coaxial, optical, USB-C, and USB-A [1][3]. It runs the company's proprietary VolumioOS platform, is Roon Ready certified, and natively supports Tidal, Qobuz, Spotify Connect, AirPlay, Bluetooth 5.0, and DLNA/UPnP [1].

## Technology Level

$$ \Large \text{0.6} $$

The hardware architecture relies on well-established components: the ESS ES9039Q2M chips are current ESS delta-sigma DAC silicon used across competing streaming DACs, while the dual-mono topology, low-jitter oscillator, and separate analog and digital linear power supplies represent mature engineering practices rather than novel advances [1][3]. The embedded ARM Cortex-A72 SoC traces to 2015-era processor IP, placing it behind current-generation platforms deployed in competing devices. No proprietary hardware patents were identified.

VolumioOS constitutes the primary technical differentiator: a long-running software platform encompassing Roon Ready integration, FusionDSP/CamillaDSP parametric DSP, multiroom synchronization, and native streaming service clients [1][3]. The hardware design is specified in-house by Volumio, with the Primo Plus described as a complete architectural redesign drawing on technology from the earlier Preciso DAC project [3]. The cited sources do not disclose a manufacturing partner. Competing streaming platforms already offer similar software function sets, and all hardware components are commercially available, so no sustained multi-year competitive advantage is evident.

## Reliability & Support

$$ \Large \text{0.7} $$

Volumio provides manufacturer-direct global support through a ticket system, community forum, and RMA process, with materials available in five languages [1]. The standard 2-year warranty covers material and manufacturing defects; software defects are explicitly excluded [1]. VolumioOS receives active over-the-air updates—multiple firmware releases were published per month in early 2026, addressing streaming SDK stability, Bluetooth connectivity, and platform improvements [2].

Physical repairs, both under warranty and out-of-warranty, route through Volumio's support process, with international customers exposed to outbound shipping and customs costs [1]. No published failure rate data exists for the Primo Plus. Construction uses solid-state storage, an external power supply that reduces internal heat, and an aluminum chassis; the 1.3-inch OLED display carries the standard long-term aging risk inherent to OLED technology.

## Rationality of Design Philosophy

$$ \Large \text{0.6} $$

The Primo Plus rests on a largely rational engineering foundation: a delta-sigma dual-mono architecture with dedicated linear power regulation for analog and digital domains, and a software platform providing parametric EQ and room correction with demonstrably audible effects on frequency response [1][3]. The product line demonstrates consistent measurable architectural progress—from the original single-DAC RCA-only Primo, through the balanced-output Primo 2, to the dual-mono dual-PSU Primo Plus with expanded connectivity—representing genuine engineering advancement each generation [3]. The product's role as a Roon Ready streaming DAC with balanced preamp outputs and integrated DSP has clear justification as dedicated audio equipment that general-purpose alternatives do not directly match.

Two elements weaken a fully measurement-optimized assessment. The inclusion of a NOS (non-oversampling) mode is a preference-oriented option: NOS degrades frequency response accuracy relative to properly implemented oversampling and is not the optimal signal processing choice. Additionally, marketing assertions that 90 femtosecond clock precision delivers "virtually jitter-free playback" and that the circuit's mirror-image layout produces audible sonic improvements constitute claims of audible benefit that are not substantiated by independent measurement under controlled conditions [1][3].

## Advice

Prospective buyers who specifically require Roon Ready certification, balanced XLR line outputs, integrated parametric DSP, and comprehensive streaming service support in a single chassis will find the Primo Plus a functionally complete solution. It is suitable as a network source driving a power amplifier or active speakers directly, eliminating a separate preamplifier. However, because no published audio performance measurements are available, the actual fidelity level remains unverified. Competing devices at lower prices publish more complete manufacturer performance specifications. Buyers without a specific need for the VolumioOS ecosystem or Italian provenance should carefully evaluate whether the Primo Plus's price premium is justified for their use case.
