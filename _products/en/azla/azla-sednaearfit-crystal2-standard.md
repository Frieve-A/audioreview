## Overview

AZLA is a South Korea-based in-ear monitor and eartip manufacturer. The SednaEarfit Crystal2 Standard is a replacement silicone ear tip made from German-sourced Liquid Silicone Rubber, offered in six sizes (SS, S, MS, M, ML, L) as 2-pair sets for 22.00 USD per size through AZLA's official store [1]. It combines a tapered bore profile intended to improve seal retention with a hexagonal debris filter at the nozzle opening [1][2]. The tip is sold globally through AZLA's own store and multiple third-party retailers.

## Rationality of Design Philosophy

$$ \Large \text{0.3} $$

The design combines a physically-grounded element—anatomy-derived sizing and a tapered seal intended to affect passive isolation and fit—with subjective marketing language such as "sound density" and "spatial audio enhancement" that carries no ABX, blind-test, or measurement evidence, which is treated as an unverified audibility claim. The overall approach is also an iterative refinement of already-common industry techniques—wide-bore tapered tips and anatomy-based sizing used by multiple competing brands—rather than a genuinely innovative approach to the eartip problem.
