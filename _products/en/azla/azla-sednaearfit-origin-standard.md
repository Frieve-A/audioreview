## Overview

AZLA is a South Korea-based eartip specialist behind the multi-generation SednaEarfit line. Origin Standard, released in 2024, updates the original 2017 SednaEarfit design with medical-grade silicone, a dual-layer retention structure, and a tapered/horn bore intended to improve fit and driver coupling [1][2]. It ships in six sizes (SS through L), designed for nozzle diameters of 5.4-7.2mm, at 12.00 USD per two-pair pack [2].

## Technology Level

$$ \Large \text{0.4} $$

AZLA is the in-house designer of the SednaEarfit line, with no evidence of OEM/ODM origin [2][3]. As a dedicated eartip maker, the company shows some specialized know-how accumulation, reflected in a multi-generation product family and a stated ergonomic dataset built from analysis of 788 ear-canal shapes [3]. However, none of the core technologies used — medical-grade silicone, tapered/horn bore geometry, and a dual-layer retention structure — are cutting-edge or patent-protected. Bore-geometry acoustic tuning was an innovative approach within the eartip category at earlier points, but is now common practice among competitors [3]. This provides no lasting competitive moat, and the approach is easy for other manufacturers to replicate. As a purely mechanical, passive accessory, the product includes no digital or software integration. The net effect is a below-average but non-trivial technology level.

## Rationality of Design Philosophy

$$ \Large \text{0.5} $$

AZLA's design approach for SednaEarfit Origin is comfort- and fit-oriented, with a secondary claimed acoustic-tuning benefit, using standard mass-produced medical-grade silicone rather than exotic materials or handcrafted processes [1][2]. This is a rational, non-wasteful approach for a simple accessory category. However, none of the manufacturer's specific claims — improved retention, reduced ear-canal pressure, and balanced frequency delivery — are independently verified by measurement data, so no credit is given for a demonstrated measurement-focused approach. The claimed improvements over the 2017 original SednaEarfit likewise lack independent verification, so no progression credit is awarded. As a simple mechanical accessory with no viable superior alternative material or technology being bypassed, no conservative-approach penalty applies. The balance of unverified-but-plausible claims against an otherwise rational, non-wasteful approach nets to a neutral score.

## Advice

SednaEarfit Origin Standard is a low-cost, six-size silicone eartip aimed at improving physical fit and retention on wired IEMs with 5.4-7.2mm nozzles. No independent measurement data confirms its claimed acoustic-tuning or comfort benefits, so these should be treated as unverified manufacturer claims rather than proven performance improvements. Given the low price and the absence of any equivalent-or-better cheaper alternative, the main practical consideration for buyers is nozzle-diameter and size compatibility with a specific IEM and ear canal, rather than measured audio performance.
