## Overview

The GRAS 45CA is a binaural acoustic test fixture from GRAS Sound & Vibration, a Danish manufacturer of precision measurement microphones and ear simulators. Used by acoustic-testing laboratories and headphone/hearing-protector manufacturers, the 45CA-10 combines IEC 60318-4-compliant high-frequency ear simulators with anthropometric pinnae and is rated for measurements up to 20 kHz [1][6]. It is a professional acoustic measurement instrument, not a product that reproduces sound for a listener.

## Scientific Validity

$$ \Large \text{0.5} $$

The manufacturer states Self Insertion Loss of more than 50 dB at 80-250 Hz, more than 65 dB at 350-4,000 Hz, and more than 55 dB at 5,000-20,000 Hz [1]. This describes the fixture's rejection of external noise during measurement, not an audible effect delivered to a listener. Public evidence does not provide third-party validation of the configured 45CA's coupler accuracy, insertion-loss repeatability, unit variation, or overall measurement uncertainty. Scientific Validity therefore cannot be evaluated beyond the neutral score from the available system-level evidence.

## Technology Level

$$ \Large \text{0.7} $$

The 45CA is designed and manufactured in-house by GRAS and reflects substantial measurement-transducer and fixture-design know-how [6]. Its core IEC 60318-1/60318-4 coupler principles are industry standards rather than proprietary standards. In the 45CA-10, the RA0402 ear simulators damp the standard coupler's 13.5 kHz resonance by about 14 dB, extend the useful range to 20 kHz, and specify a ±2.2 dB transfer-impedance tolerance from 10 to 20 kHz [7]. The published graph shows the resonance peak reduced from roughly 36 dB to about 23 dB [7]. TEDS calibration-data support is appropriate contemporary integration [6]. No public patent basis or independent competitor benchmark establishes a durable technical moat, so the score recognizes in-house design and specialized know-how without assuming one.

## Cost-Performance

$$ \Large \text{0.1} $$

Current market price of the review target (45CA-10 configuration): 18,684 USD (26,540 CAD) [2]. The cheapest identified fixture with equivalent-or-better user-facing functions is the miniDSP EARS Pro at 1,800 USD, including its bundled measurement interface [3][4].

CP = 1,800 USD / 18,684 USD = 0.096

Both fixtures are equipped with a binaural, IEC 60318-4 compliant coupler, anthropometric pinnae, and individual factory calibration; the EARS Pro additionally bundles a ready-to-use USB interface not included in the 45CA's priced configuration [3][4]. On measured performance: the EARS Pro is specified for a frequency response measurement range of 20 Hz–20 kHz ±1 dB with its calibration file loaded [4], matching the 45CA-10's rated measurement capability to 20 kHz via its high-frequency ear simulator [2]. The 45CA's manufacturer-specified Self Insertion Loss (80–250 Hz: >50 dB; 350–4000 Hz: >65 dB; 5000–20000 Hz: >55 dB) [1] has no published equivalent for the EARS Pro; since the 45CA's own figure is itself only a manufacturer specification rather than a third-party measurement, this gap is treated as provisional and non-disqualifying for both sides. On a manufacturer-spec-to-manufacturer-spec basis, the comparator's functions and measured performance are equivalent-or-better, yielding a Cost-Performance score of 0.1.

## Reliability & Support

$$ \Large \text{0.8} $$

The 45CA carries a 5-year warranty on the main assembly, with cables separately warrantied for 6 months [1]. Support includes manufacturer repair and calibration services and a dedicated United States service center operated by DGI Metrology [5], alongside GRAS operations in Denmark. This provides specialized, multi-region metrology support rather than relying on a single sales dealer. No statistical failure-rate or long-duration field data is published, so population failure rate and long-term durability remain neutral.

## Rationality of Design Philosophy

$$ \Large \text{0.9} $$

The 45CA is purpose-built measurement equipment whose design language is standards compliance and measurement accuracy, with no subjective sound-quality claims in its documentation [6]. Cost is concentrated in performance-relevant elements: precision-machined couplers and ear simulators, calibrated microphones, and vibration-isolating mounting. The high-frequency variant reduces the standard simulator's 13.5 kHz resonance by about 14 dB and extends the useful range to 20 kHz [7], a measurable model progression with a functional benefit. As dedicated test equipment, its existence is justified because general-purpose or non-standardized rigs do not reproduce the specified IEC/ISO coupler and fixture geometry [6].

## Advice

The 45CA is suited to laboratories and manufacturers that require standards-compliant, individually calibrated binaural measurement of headphones, earphones, and hearing protectors, particularly where IEEE 1451.4 TEDS workflows, ISO 4869-3 insertion-loss work, and 20 kHz measurement bandwidth are required. The lower-cost miniDSP EARS Pro can be considered for headphone-only work, but not as a like-for-like replacement: its official specification does not claim ISO 4869-3 hearing-protector testing or the 45CA's large-baseplate leakage control, and a published comparison reports a 13 kHz resonance and treble differences from a GRAS RA0402-based fixture [3][4]. Confirm the required standards, application, specific 45CA variant, and ear-simulator/microphone combination before purchase.
