## Overview

The Maestraudio MA910SB is a wired in-ear monitor produced entirely in Japan by O2AID Corporation (Takasaki, Gunma Prefecture) under the Maestraudio brand, distributed domestically by Aiuto Co., Ltd. Released in April 2023 as the 4.4mm balanced-connection variant of the MA910S, it incorporates O2AID's proprietary RST (Reactive Sympathetic Tweeter) — a passive ceramic-coated tweeter that resonates sympathetically with the main driver's rear radiation — alongside a licensed HDSS (High Definition Sound Standard) acoustic correction module and a 10mm graphene-coated dynamic driver. The cable is fixed and non-detachable at a 4.4mm balanced L-shaped plug. Current Japanese market price converts to 44 USD for English scoring [1][2].

## Scientific Validity

$$ \Large \text{0.5} $$

Manufacturer specifications indicate a frequency response range of 20Hz–40kHz and a sensitivity of 102 dB, with no ±dB deviation tolerance or distortion figures published [1]. No independent third-party measurements are available from any credible measurement source; Scientific Validity cannot be evaluated and is set to 0.5.

## Technology Level

$$ \Large \text{0.7} $$

The official product page documents a non-commodity acoustic stack: a proprietary RST passive ceramic-coated tweeter, a dedicated 10mm graphene-coated dynamic driver, and HDSS, a licensed TBI Audio Systems LLC acoustic correction technology [1]. This is more technically distinctive than a basic single-dynamic-driver IEM. Two factors limit the score: the MA910SB's advancement over its predecessor (the MA910S) is limited to a connector change from 3.5mm SE to 4.4mm balanced with incremental retuning, leaving the housing, driver array, and acoustic architecture unchanged; and the entire technology stack is pure passive analog/acoustic with no digital integration, DSP, or software-defined processing.

## Cost-Performance

$$ \Large \text{0.7} $$

This site evaluates based solely on functionality and measured performance values, without considering driver types or configurations.

The Maestraudio MA910SB is priced at 44 USD equivalent in the Japanese domestic market [1][2]. The cheapest identified equivalent-or-better comparator after normalizing the 4.4mm connection requirement is a 7Hz Salnotes Zero no-microphone IEM at 20.99 USD plus a Tripowin Karen 4.4 / 2PIN cable at 9.99 USD, for a total of 30.98 USD [3][4]. This bundle provides passive wired IEM listening, no wireless/DSP dependency, 0.78mm 2-pin detachable cabling, and a 4.4mm balanced source connection; compared with the MA910SB's fixed 4.4mm plug, it closes the connector function while improving cable serviceability.

## Reliability & Support

$$ \Large \text{0.3} $$

The MA910SB carries a 1-year warranty from date of purchase, and post-discontinuation parts availability is limited to one year after manufacturing ends [1]. Support is handled exclusively through Aiuto Co., Ltd. in Japan; purchases through gray-market or international channels receive no warranty coverage and must rely on third-party repair services. No known systematic failure patterns or documented hardware recalls have been identified as of this review date. The product's passive two-driver construction has inherently low electrical complexity. The fixed non-detachable cable means any cable failure requires full unit return for repair.

## Rationality of Design Philosophy

$$ \Large \text{0.2} $$

The MA910SB is built on passive acoustic engineering: ceramic sympathetic resonance (RST), a licensed ETL acoustic correction module (HDSS), and a graphene-coated dynamic driver [1]. These rest on coherent physical principles — sympathetic resonance and ETL acoustic energy dissipation are real phenomena, not marketing abstractions. However, three aspects reduce the score. First, the silver-coated OFC hybrid cable is specifically claimed to enhance sound separation and localization — an effect for which no ABX evidence has been presented, constituting a claim of audible improvement without scientific substantiation [1]. Second, the product includes non-acoustic value elements such as a genuine leather cable reel and color options; these may matter for ownership experience but do not independently demonstrate measured acoustic improvement [1]. Third, the design entirely forgoes measurement-referenced acoustic optimization already commercially deployed by competing manufacturers before the April 2023 release date, resulting in no published frequency response targets and no means of objectively validating the RST and HDSS performance claims. The RST concept is a novel acoustic idea, but novelty without independent verification does not constitute rational design.

## Advice

Buyers for whom Japanese domestic assembly and a fixed 4.4mm balanced connection are primary requirements will find the MA910SB a product with documented engineering differentiation. However, the complete absence of published acoustic performance data beyond a bare frequency response range means the benefits of the RST and HDSS technologies cannot be independently confirmed. The 1-year warranty and Japan-only support infrastructure limit long-term serviceability for buyers outside Japan. Passive IEM alternatives with available third-party measurement data and 4.4mm connectivity are accessible at lower total cost; the 7Hz bundle cited above is the documented example [3][4][5]. Buyers without a specific requirement for Maestraudio's domestic assembly or RST/HDSS architecture should consider measured alternatives before committing to the MA910SB.
