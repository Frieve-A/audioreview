## Overview

The MAPro1000 II is a wired in-ear monitor developed by Outzed Co. and released by Ayuto Co., Ltd. on November 1, 2025 as a revision of the original MAPro1000 [6]. It pairs a 10mm graphene-coated dynamic driver with a proprietary 5.8mm passive ceramic-coated RST tweeter and a detachable MMCX cable terminated with a 3.5mm plug. The II revision changes the internal solder and MMCX contact construction, specifies 20Hz–40,000Hz, and is assembled in Japan [1]. Its current Japanese market price is equivalent to 54.77 USD at the cited ECB reference rate [2][7].

## Scientific Validity

$$ \Large \text{0.5} $$

Manufacturer specifications state 20Hz–40,000Hz without a deviation tolerance, 111dB SPL/mW sensitivity, and 22-ohm impedance [1]. The sensitivity indicates no obvious drive-level limitation for ordinary IEM use, but it does not establish fidelity. No credible independent frequency-response, THD, or isolation measurement is currently available for this model. Frequency-response error, distortion, channel matching, and isolation therefore remain unknown, so the available evidence supports a mid-level Scientific Validity score rather than a favorable one.

## Technology Level

$$ \Large \text{0.5} $$

Outzed develops Maestraudio products in Japan by combining acoustic and vibration analysis with piezoelectric-ceramic design, and identifies VST and RST as proprietary developments [5]. For the MAPro1000 II, the 5.8mm RST is optimized for the small enclosure through acoustic and vibration simulation [1]. This demonstrates in-house design ownership and specialized implementation knowledge. However, the RST platform dates to the 2022 MA910S, while graphene-coated diaphragms and silver-content solder are mature techniques [1]. The product remains a purely analog/mechanical design without digital or software integration. Proprietary transducer expertise is balanced by mature component technologies and limited system integration, supporting a middle-level result.

## Cost-Performance

$$ \Large \text{0.4} $$

This site evaluates based solely on functionality and measured performance values, without considering driver types or configurations. The MAPro1000 II's current Japanese market price converts to 54.77 USD [2][7]. It provides a detachable MMCX cable, a 3.5mm single-ended plug, and passive isolation, with no DSP, ANC, or wireless functionality.

The currently purchasable 7Hz Salnotes Zero costs 20.99 USD [4]. It uses a different connector standard but provides the same user-facing functions: a detachable cable, 3.5mm plug, passive isolation, and included eartips, without DSP, ANC, or wireless functions. Audio Science Review's graph, referenced at 425Hz and 94dB SPL, shows left and right channels closely aligned below 10kHz. Relative to its target, response is approximately 1–2.5dB low at 20–100Hz, closely aligned at 200–800Hz, 0–1.5dB high at 1–5kHz, and 1–4dB low at 5–10kHz; the source warns that the 12.7kHz peak is a fixture artifact [3]. THD at 94dB SPL is generally 0.02–0.07%, with an approximately 0.1% feature near 1kHz; even at 104dB SPL, most of the band remains below approximately 0.3% [3].

Because the MAPro1000 II lacks equivalent target-error, THD, channel-matching, and isolation measurements, equivalence remains provisional. Salnotes Zero nevertheless has equal user-facing functions and documented target adherence, channel matching, and low distortion on every performance axis for which usable evidence exists.

## Reliability & Support

$$ \Large \text{0.4} $$

The MAPro1000 II carries a 1-year warranty on the earphone body, 6 months on the cable, and DOA-only coverage on accessories [1]. Ayuto publishes these terms for its Japanese warranty channel; the cited page does not state international coverage. No published statistical failure-rate or MTBF data was identified for this model. The II adds an MMCX contact-correction washer that the manufacturer says improves connection reliability [1], though no independent long-term data yet confirms improved durability. The short warranty period is the clearest reliability factor for this product.

## Rationality of Design Philosophy

$$ \Large \text{0.4} $$

Maestraudio frames the MAPro1000 II around "unexaggerated faithfulness" and flat monitor-style tuning [1], which is a rational fidelity-oriented objective. The revised MMCX construction is also directed at a practical connection point. However, the product invests in a graphene-coated diaphragm, a separate piezoelectric tweeter, and silver-content internal solder without adding a user-facing function beyond that of an ordinary passive IEM. In particular, the manufacturer attributes improved signal transmission, transient rise, high-frequency reproduction, and nuance to the solder [1], implying audible benefits that are not scientifically expected over such short internal wiring. The fidelity objective and connector revision are rational elements, but the inaudible solder claim and component complexity without a demonstrated functional benefit keep the overall direction below average.

## Advice

The MAPro1000 II provides a passive wired format with a detachable MMCX cable, but no independent measurement currently confirms its flat-tuning claim. Its warranty is limited to 1 year for the earphone and 6 months for the cable, and buyers outside Japan should confirm eligibility because the cited terms do not state international coverage. Buyers prioritizing confirmed measured performance should consider the 20.99 USD 7Hz Salnotes Zero, which provides equivalent user-facing functions plus documented channel matching, low distortion, and close target adherence. The manufacturer's tonal and solder claims should remain unverified until controlled measurements become available.
