## Overview

The HiBy Music Yacht10 is the inaugural model in HiBy's "voyage & discovery" IEM series, released in early 2025 at 129 USD [1]. It uses a single 10mm dynamic driver with a magnesium-aluminum alloy dome composite diaphragm in a CNC-machined T6063 aluminum alloy shell styled in a cyberpunk/mecha aesthetic. The standard package includes a Litz Type 4 PCOCC cable with a 4-pin lock adapter system providing both 3.5mm single-ended and 4.4mm balanced terminations. HiBy, primarily a manufacturer of digital audio players and DAC/amp devices, introduces this as the entry point of a new standalone IEM line.

## Scientific Validity

$$ \Large \text{0.5} $$

Manufacturer specifications [1] state a frequency response of 20–40,000Hz (no deviation tolerance specified), THD of 0.045% at 500Hz and 0.098% at 1kHz at 94dB SPL, sensitivity of 95dB at 1kHz, and impedance of 24Ω. The 500Hz THD figure represents good distortion performance; the 1kHz figure of 0.098% falls within an acceptable range for an earphone. No independent calibrated measurement data from third-party sources is available to verify these specifications.

## Technology Level

$$ \Large \text{0.3} $$

HiBy describes in-house design with a dedicated R&D team and no identified OEM driver supplier, representing a real design investment. However, every constituent technology in the Yacht10 — the Mg-Al alloy dome with polymer suspension diaphragm, dual magnetic core circuit (1.2T flux), H.CCAW voice coil, tuned metal rear chamber (marketed as L.S.W.), CNC-machined aluminum housing, and air-pressure valve — is established industry practice widely adopted across the IEM market since 2020–2022 [1]. No IEM-specific patents were identified. The technology stack carries no sustainable competitive advantage: equivalent configurations are commercially available from numerous manufacturers, and no licensing or know-how barrier prevents replication. The product is fully passive with no digital processing, software integration, or AI involvement, representing one of the most conservative integration choices possible in the 2025 IEM market. The proprietary 4-pin lock adapter system is a functional convenience feature but is neither patented nor cross-compatible with competing modular systems.

## Cost-Performance

$$ \Large \text{0.2} $$

The cost-performance comparison below treats driver type and configuration as non-scoring internal design choices; it compares user-facing functions and measured performance.

The Yacht10 provides passive IEM playback, 3.5mm SE connectivity (included cable), 4.4mm balanced connectivity (via included 4-pin lock adapter), and a detachable 0.78mm 2-pin cable.

The Truthear GATE's official product page documents a 0.78mm 2-pin detachable cable interface [3]. ShenzhenAudio lists the GATE without microphone at 21.99 USD [4]. Adding the Moondrop OLD FASHIONED only-cable 4.4mm option at 9.99 USD [4] closes the balanced-termination gap, for a normalized total of 31.98 USD. The GATE demonstrates equivalent-or-better performance on the available measurement evidence:

- THD: Yacht10 0.098% at 1kHz (manufacturer specification [1]); GATE was reported by Audio Science Review as essentially distortionless at 104 dBSPL [2], so the independently measured comparator evidence is at least equivalent to the unverified Yacht10 claim
- Frequency response: Yacht10 publishes only 20–40,000Hz without deviation tolerance [1]; GATE has Audio Science Review target-comparison measurement data [2] and official effective 20Hz–20kHz range [3]. Because Yacht10 lacks calibrated deviation data, GATE is the stronger verified performance reference

This comparison is provisional, as the Yacht10 has manufacturer specifications only; results should be revised when independent measurement data becomes available.

## Reliability & Support

$$ \Large \text{0.6} $$

The Yacht10 carries a 12-month warranty from date of purchase [5], shorter than the two-year coverage listed for the comparator GATE by ShenzhenAudio [4]. The passive single dynamic driver architecture is inherently resistant to failure: no active electronics, a robust CNC-machined T6063 aluminum alloy housing, and a replaceable detachable cable that can be swapped independently. HiBy provides manufacturer-direct support internationally with offices in Dongguan, China and Lewes, USA, and documented email-based after-sales channels. No physical defects, recalls, or service bulletins have been documented for this model as of 2026-07-05. Post-warranty parts supply duration and an authorized service center network are not publicly documented.

## Rationality of Design Philosophy

$$ \Large \text{0.3} $$

Current IEM options include measurement-tuned single dynamic driver designs, planar magnetic IEMs, and multi-driver hybrid configurations, many with published independent measurement data. Against this landscape, the Yacht10 adopts a fully passive single-driver approach with no DSP, no app integration, and no active features of any kind [1] — despite HiBy possessing demonstrated software expertise through HiByOS and the HiBy Music app. The primary differentiating investment is the cyberpunk/mecha aesthetic (CNC carving, hand polishing, custom colorways, and the "voyage & discovery" concept) rather than a measurably justified acoustic rationale for the passive-only design. Marketing materials attribute dynamics and openness improvements to the L.S.W. rear chamber without accompanying acoustic measurement data [1]. The PCOCC Litz cable is also presented as an audio quality contributor, though cable-level acoustic contribution at audio frequencies is not supported by controlled evidence of audibility. These represent claims of audible benefits that are not supported by independent measurement data.

## Advice

The Yacht10's build package is materially robust: the CNC-machined aluminum housing is corrosion-resistant, and the included modular cable with both 3.5mm and 4.4mm terminations provides practical convenience. However, no independent measurement data currently verifies the manufacturer's distortion specifications, and the cited manufacturer data do not include calibrated frequency-response deviation, so neutral tuning is not independently verified. The 1-year warranty is shorter than the two-year coverage listed for the comparator GATE [4][5]. Buyers who prioritize independently verified performance will find alternatives such as the Truthear GATE at 21.99 USD, with optional 4.4mm cable normalization still below 32 USD, and Audio Science Review-confirmed measurements [2][4] indicating equivalent-or-better distortion evidence at a substantially lower cost. Those drawn primarily to the build aesthetic and HiBy ecosystem integration will find the construction quality substantial; buyers focused on measured audio performance should review lower-cost alternatives with published independent data before committing.
