## Overview

The Master & Dynamic ME05 is a passive wired in-ear earphone, originally released in 2015 and relaunched in December 2024. The 2024 model replaces the titanium diaphragm with an 8mm bio-cellulose dynamic driver, adds a bundled USB-C to 3.5mm DAC adapter supporting up to 32-bit/384kHz, and integrates an updated MEMS microphone with a three-button inline remote. The housing is precision-machined solid brass with hand-polished accents. Retail price is 199 USD [1].

## Scientific Validity

$$ \Large \text{0.5} $$

Third-party measurements from InnerFidelity document the 2015 ME05 (titanium driver), showing frequency response deviation of 2.90 dB from the Harman IEM target [2]. The 2024 relaunch uses a redesigned bio-cellulose driver; no independent third-party measurements of the current model are available, so the 2015 data is applied provisionally with inherent uncertainty regarding the updated driver's acoustic characteristics. THD is presented only as plotted curves in the InnerFidelity PDF, with no source table or single numeric summary available for direct extraction. The manufacturer publishes no frequency response, THD, or sensitivity specifications [1]. Because current-model measurement evidence is missing and the 2015 data is not a direct measurement of the relaunched driver, Scientific Validity cannot be fully evaluated and the score remains neutral.

## Technology Level

$$ \Large \text{0.1} $$

The ME05 employs no proprietary or novel technology. Bio-cellulose dynamic drivers have been commercially established since the early 2000s and are in use across numerous manufacturers. Brass earphone enclosures have been standard industry practice for decades. The bundled USB-C to 3.5mm DAC adapter is a common accessory approach used industry-wide since 2016. OFC cables and MEMS microphones are commodity components throughout the market. No patent, technical-paper, manufacturing-infrastructure, or supplier disclosure is identified for core ME05 technology; the sole proprietary claim — a "wind-rejection acoustic mesh" on the microphone — is presented without patent citation or independent verification [1]. Publicly documented in-house contribution is limited to the enclosure's industrial design. The 2024 relaunch represents a minor compatibility update to a decade-old product — a driver material change with no published acoustic improvement data at an unchanged retail price — reflecting stagnation rather than advancement. The complete technology stack requires no licensing and can be replicated by any competitor.

## Cost-Performance

$$ \Large \text{0.1} $$

This site evaluates based solely on functionality and measured performance values, without considering driver types or configurations.

The ME05 is priced at 199 USD [1]. The Moondrop Quarks with mic (17.99 USD at Shenzhenaudio [4]) combined with the Apple USB-C to 3.5 mm Headphone Jack Adapter (9.00 USD [5]) provides equivalent-or-better core listening and calling functionality at a normalized total of 26.99 USD. The Quarks with mic delivers 3.5mm wired connectivity and an inline microphone, while the Apple adapter supplies the USB-C-to-3.5mm conversion included with the ME05.

Measured performance comparison:
- Frequency response deviation from Harman IEM 2019 target: 2.04 dB (Quarks, SoundStage! Network measurement [3]) vs. 2.90 dB (ME05, InnerFidelity measurement, 2015 model — provisional [2]); Quarks demonstrates better compliance
- THD: both sources provide plotted distortion data under different formats and test conditions; no directly comparable numeric table is available, so THD is not used to offset the Quarks' lower price and better Harman-target deviation

CP = 26.99 USD ÷ 199 USD = 0.14

## Reliability & Support

$$ \Large \text{0.4} $$

The US warranty period is 1 year from purchase, while 2 years applies in the European Union [6]. Manufacturer-direct support is provided globally through an online portal. The solid brass housing is inherently robust against physical deformation. However, the non-detachable cable is a concrete structural concern: the cable is permanently fixed to the housing, so cable failure cannot be remedied by the user and requires manufacturer repair or full product replacement. No IPX water or dust resistance rating is specified [1]. No statistical failure rate data or aggregate support outcome data is publicly available. No firmware considerations apply; the ME05 is a fully passive earphone with no digital firmware component.

## Rationality of Design Philosophy

$$ \Large \text{0.1} $$

Master & Dynamic's design approach for the ME05 prioritizes premium materials and brand aesthetics over acoustic measurement and scientific optimization. No frequency response targets, measurement infrastructure, or alignment with research-derived listening preference models are documented by the company. Cost allocation is heavily weighted toward brass machining, hand-polishing, and luxury lifestyle brand positioning rather than acoustic engineering. Multiple product claims lack independent verification: brass enclosures are described as providing "unique acoustic qualities" with no supporting measurement data; OFC cables carry no demonstrated audible advantage at the cable lengths and frequencies involved; the bundled "hi-resolution" USB-C DAC specification (32-bit/384kHz) is technically accurate but operationally misleading given the near-total absence of consumer content at these resolutions [1]. The 2024 relaunch is framed as a heritage revival of a decade-old product at an unchanged price, with no published acoustic improvement data accompanying the driver update — a posture that reflects brand continuity as the organizing principle rather than measurable performance advancement. All performance-focused solid-state alternatives were well established at the time of the 2024 relaunch.

## Advice

Users whose primary criterion is measured acoustic performance will find the 199 USD price substantially disproportionate to outcomes: equivalent or better measured performance with equivalent functionality is available for 26.99 USD. The permanently attached cable is a specific durability risk — cable damage cannot be addressed by the user and requires manufacturer-level repair or replacement.

The ME05 is appropriate only for buyers who specifically value the precision-machined brass construction and Master & Dynamic brand identity as primary purchase factors, and who accept the reliability trade-off of a non-replaceable cable. Anyone prioritizing measured acoustic performance should direct their budget toward products delivering superior or equivalent measured performance at a substantially lower price.
