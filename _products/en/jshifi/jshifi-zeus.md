## Overview

JSHiFi Zeus is an 8-core aftermarket IEM upgrade cable built from single-crystal copper conductors with silver plating, hand-braided and offered in 2-pin, MMCX, QDC, and CIEM 2-pin earphone-side connectors paired with 2.5mm, 3.5mm, and 4.4mm balanced plug terminations [1]. Released around January 2024 and priced at 28.00 USD [1][3], it sits as a mid-tier model within JSHiFi's re-cable lineup [2]. JSHiFi is a China-based accessory vendor that markets its cables exclusively through Amazon.co.jp and other Japanese marketplace storefronts, with no dedicated official brand website [2].

## Advice

Buyers seeking an IEM upgrade cable with the same 2-pin, MMCX, and full 2.5mm/3.5mm/4.4mm plug coverage can obtain equivalent-or-better functionality from the TRN T2 Pro at roughly 56% of the price [4]. No verifiable measurement data supports any audible benefit from JSHiFi Zeus's single-crystal copper or silver-plated conductors, so the purchase decision should rest on connector and plug compatibility rather than claimed sound-quality improvements. The cable's simple, electronics-free construction is inherently reliable, but published warranty and support information is limited, so buyers should not expect formal manufacturer backing beyond standard marketplace order protections.
