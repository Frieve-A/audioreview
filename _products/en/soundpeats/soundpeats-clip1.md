## Overview

The SoundPEATS Clip1, released November 2025, is an open-ear clip-on TWS earphone positioned at 69.99 USD on Amazon [2] and 89.99 USD through the manufacturer's official store [1]. It uses a 12mm dual-magnet dynamic driver, a BES2710 SoC supporting Bluetooth 5.4 and LDAC, and a nickel-titanium alloy clip structure (N-Flex Arch). The open-ear form factor places the driver adjacent to the outer ear without canal insertion, permitting situational awareness at the inherent cost of sound isolation.

## Scientific Validity

$$ \Large \text{0.4} $$

The Amazon product listing specifies a frequency response range of 20–40,000 Hz without a ±dB tolerance [2]. THD and S/N ratio are not published by the manufacturer. No independent third-party measurements are available for this product as of 2026-07-05. The open-ear clip-on design results in effectively 0 dB passive sound isolation: the driver sits adjacent to the ear without an acoustic seal, leaving environmental noise unattenuated. With only a tolerance-unqualified frequency response range as the available audio specification, and absent passive isolation as the sole concrete performance limitation, verified fidelity performance remains limited.

## Technology Level

$$ \Large \text{0.4} $$

The Clip1 is an in-house SOUNDPEATS design: the N-Flex Arch clip structure in 0.6mm nickel-titanium alloy and the firmware feature set (DynamicEQ Pro, AeroVoice, AutoSense) represent product-level engineering. The core SoC is the BES2710 from Bestechnic, a standard TWS chip available to any manufacturer. AeroVoice is marketed as patented, but the cited product pages do not provide a patent number [2]. LDAC (licensed from Sony, widely available since 2017) and Dolby Audio are third-party licensed features rather than in-house developments. Competitors in the same category already ship Bluetooth 6.0, LDAC, and 40-hour total battery claims [3], indicating that the Clip1's differentiators are readily replicable. The branded DSP algorithms (DynamicEQ Pro, AeroVoice) offer no independently verified performance advantage over conventional DSP, functioning primarily as product differentiation rather than confirmed technical innovation.

## Cost-Performance

$$ \Large \text{1.0} $$

The Clip1 is available at 69.99 USD on Amazon [2]. Available manufacturer/listing specifications: frequency response range 20–40,000 Hz (no ±dB tolerance stated) and passive sound isolation effectively 0 dB (open-ear design). No third-party acoustic measurements are available for this product or any evaluated candidate; all comparisons below are provisional based on manufacturer-published specifications only.

Below 69.99 USD, the relevant open-ear clip-on TWS candidate with LDAC and multipoint is the Tronsmart OpenFly Clip 2 (39.99 USD), but its total battery life with case is 32 hours — 20% below the Clip1's 40 hours — a gap no accessory bundle can close [4]. The EarFun Clip 2 meets all function criteria at 79.99 USD but is priced above the review target [3].

No lower-priced equivalent-or-better product was identified in the checked current market sources. The SoundPEATS Clip1 is the least expensive confirmed option combining open-ear clip-on form factor, LDAC, 40-hour total battery with case, multipoint, and app EQ. CP = 1.0.

## Reliability & Support

$$ \Large \text{0.7} $$

The Clip1 carries a 1-year limited warranty [1]. An optional additional year via online registration is available but is not included at purchase. The clip-on structure has minimal moving parts: the nickel-titanium arch is designed for repeated flexing without fatigue set, and no hinge mechanism is present, reducing structural failure points. SOUNDPEATS provides manufacturer-direct global support through its official store and authorized retail channels. The PeatsAudio app provides firmware update support [1]. No product recalls or widespread hardware defect reports were identified as of 2026-07-05. APH Networks reported the charging case as less sturdy than the earphones themselves, though this is an accessory item rather than a structural defect in the core product [5].

## Rationality of Design Philosophy

$$ \Large \text{0.6} $$

The Clip1's design employs current-generation components throughout: a BES2710 SoC with onboard NPU for DSP processing, licensed LDAC codec, Bluetooth 5.4, USB-C, and app-controlled EQ. Cost allocation is directed primarily at functional elements — chip, codec licensing, nitinol arch, and firmware development — with a moderate brand and software-feature premium. The progression over predecessor models (PearlClip Pro, Pop Clip) includes LDAC codec support and Bluetooth 5.4, representing tangible functional advances for users.

Several product claims are made without independent verification. DynamicEQ Pro's frequency balance improvement is asserted without controlled measurement data. Dolby Audio is marketed as delivering "immersive 3D sound" yet cannot be active simultaneously with LDAC. Battery life claimed at 40 hours measured closer to 35 hours in third-party testing at 50% volume [5]. These unverified benefit claims reduce the overall rationality of the product's design communication.

## Advice

The Clip1 is the lowest-priced confirmed open-ear clip-on TWS earphone with LDAC, 40-hour total battery with case, dual-device multipoint, and app EQ available as of mid-2026. The open-ear design provides effectively 0 dB of passive isolation; buyers requiring any degree of environmental noise attenuation should select a sealed or over-ear product instead. Enabling LDAC reduces per-charge playtime to 4 hours, requiring case recharging for extended listening sessions [2]. The 40-hour total battery claim should be treated as an upper bound; actual usage closer to 35 hours has been observed in at least one battery test [5]. No independent acoustic measurements are publicly available, so frequency response accuracy and distortion performance cannot be independently confirmed before purchase. The base warranty is 1 year, and the charging case has been reported as a durability concern. This product fits users who prioritize open-ear comfort, LDAC wireless connectivity, and feature completeness over independently verified acoustic performance.
