## Overview

ThieAudio was launched in 2019 as a creative endeavor of Linsoul Audio, with project-based engineer selection described in its official product materials [1]. The Legacy 2 is a 99 USD wired hybrid IEM using a Knowles 29689 balanced armature and a 10 mm beryllium dynamic driver with a 3.5 mm cable and 0.78 mm 2-pin connector [1]. Within ThieAudio's Legacy line it sits below the Legacy 3, 4, 5, and 9 models listed in the same series navigation [1].

## Scientific Validity

$$ \Large \text{0.7} $$

The cited official page gives driver and connection specifications but does not publish THD, S/N ratio, or independently testable acoustic tolerances [1]. Third-party FR measurements from Crinacle's IEC-711 coupler database show a deviation of 1.75 dB STD from the Harman 2019 IEM target [2], indicating good tonal accuracy. The plotted response shows sub-bass emphasis, controlled midrange, and upper-treble structure typical of IEC-711/60318-4 data; high-Q 8-9 kHz features should be treated cautiously because coupler resonance can dominate this region. No Legacy2 THD or noise data is cited here, so the 0.7 score rests mainly on the available FR result rather than a complete measurement suite.

## Technology Level

$$ \Large \text{0.1} $$

The Legacy 2 is an analog passive hybrid built from commercial driver types: a Knowles 29689 balanced armature plus a 10 mm beryllium dynamic driver [1]. The cited official material emphasizes selected components and tuning, but it does not document patents, proprietary DSP, software control, active correction, or a novel integration method [1]. Passive acoustic crossover and 3.5 mm wired operation are mature IEM design patterns, and the architecture can be reproduced by competitors using standard suppliers. There is no digital, software, or computational integration of any kind, which supports a low technology score.

## Cost-Performance

$$ \Large \text{0.2} $$

For this comparison, driver count and material names are not counted as functions by themselves; the relevant user-facing functions are passive wired IEM operation, detachable cable, and measured acoustic output.

The Legacy2 is priced at 99 USD [1]. The 7Hz Salnotes Zero, available at 20.99 USD (Linsoul) [4], is a passive 3.5 mm IEM with a detachable 0.78 mm 2-pin cable and no active processing, giving it the same user-facing functions as the Legacy2. Its FR deviation from the Harman 2019 IEM target measures 1.31 dB STD, compared to 1.75 dB STD for the Legacy2 [3], indicating better measured tonal accuracy. Audio Science Review's plotted measurements show low THD for the Salnotes Zero [3]; the cited Legacy2 sources do not include corresponding THD data, so the comparison rests mainly on FR, equivalent functions, and price. Passive isolation is not independently measured for either product; both are passive in-canal IEMs.

## Reliability & Support

$$ \Large \text{0.6} $$

The official page confirms passive wired construction with a detachable 0.78 mm 2-pin cable and 3.5 mm plug [1]. This avoids battery, firmware, charging, and active-electronics failure modes, while leaving cable wear user-replaceable. The cited official source does not disclose a product-specific warranty term or repair policy for Legacy2, and no recall or service-warning notice is present on the official product page [1]. Overall support visibility is limited, but passive construction keeps the reliability risk moderate.

## Rationality of Design Philosophy

$$ \Large \text{0.3} $$

ThieAudio's official product copy emphasizes enjoyable tuning, technical performance, driver selection, and hand-selected engineers [1], but it does not cite measurement targets, ABX testing, or objective tuning methodology. The product uses a conventional passive 1DD+1BA topology, no DSP, no active correction, and hand-finished resin/faceplate work that mainly affects appearance rather than measured output [1]. By current design standards, cost allocated to cosmetic handwork and analog-only integration provides limited functional benefit compared with measurement-led or DSP-assisted approaches. This supports a low rationality score.

## Advice

The Legacy2 delivers a 1.75 dB STD frequency response deviation from the Harman 2019 IEM target, a good result for a passive IEM. However, the 7Hz Salnotes Zero achieves 1.31 dB STD FR deviation with low THD shown in Audio Science Review's plots [3] at 20.99 USD [4], giving it stronger cited measurements against the Legacy2's 99 USD asking price. Because the cited Legacy2 sources do not include THD/noise data or a product-specific warranty term, buyers prioritizing measured performance and documentation should select the Salnotes Zero or comparable alternatives.
