## Overview

The F.Audio FA4 is a portable touchscreen digital audio player built around dual ES9038Q2M DACs. It provides standalone microSD playback, USB DAC operation, and bidirectional Bluetooth. The product images show native 2.5 mm and 4.4 mm balanced outputs, separate 3.5 mm headphone and line outputs, USB-C, and a microSD slot [1][2]. The current US price is 146.88 USD with an active add-to-cart listing [1].

## Scientific Validity

$$ \Large \text{0.5} $$

No credible independent FA4 bench measurements or graphs were identified. Seller documentation quotes output power of approximately 280/350 mW single-ended and 390/460 mW balanced in low/high gain, but gives no load, distortion limit, bandwidth, or test method [1]. It likewise provides no controlled THD/THD+N, SINAD, frequency-response, crosstalk, output-impedance, or noise measurement. These uncontrolled figures cannot establish audible fidelity or even comparable power at a specified load. Scientific Validity therefore cannot be evaluated from the available data and remains neutral.

## Technology Level

$$ \Large \text{0.4} $$

The FA4 combines mature, commercially available dual ES9038Q2M DACs and a Bluetooth platform with a touchscreen interface, local-file playback, USB DAC operation, bidirectional Bluetooth, and balanced analog outputs [1][2]. Integrating these functions and their firmware into a portable player requires competent product engineering, but the cited sources identify no patent, technical paper, proprietary silicon, or feature that creates a durable barrier to replication. The implementation is more substantial than a generic single-function device, but its established components and readily reproducible architecture keep Technology Level below current leading designs.

## Cost-Performance

$$ \Large \text{1.0} $$

The FA4's current US price is 146.88 USD [1]. The closest lower-priced candidate is the Shanling M0 Pro at 129 USD plus its required 15.99 USD 5-pin 3.5 mm-to-4.4 mm balanced adapter, for a 144.99 USD bundle [3][4]. It matches standalone microSD playback, USB DAC mode, and bidirectional Bluetooth, and its specified balanced S/N ratio is 119 dB at 32 ohms. It provides 236 mW at 32 ohms balanced and 90 mW at 32 ohms single-ended; the FA4 seller quotes higher 460/350 mW maxima without a load, so power equivalence cannot be established. The M0 Pro also lacks the FA4's native 2.5 mm balanced and separate 3.5 mm line-output sockets, and its DSD128 ceiling is below the FA4 seller's stated USB DoP DSD256 and local-app DSD512 support [1][3]. It is therefore not a confirmed equal-or-better comparator.

No cheaper finished player was confirmed to match the FA4's local playback, USB DAC, bidirectional Bluetooth, native single-ended/2.5 mm/4.4 mm outputs, separate line output, format support, and available output-power claims. The FA4 is the cheapest confirmed option with its complete function set, so Cost-Performance is 1.0.

With support terms largely undocumented and no evidence that service has ended, Reliability & Support is slightly below neutral.

## Rationality of Design Philosophy

$$ \Large \text{0.6} $$

The FA4 directs its hardware and software toward user-facing functions: standalone storage playback, a touchscreen library interface, USB DAC mode, bidirectional Bluetooth, and single-ended plus balanced outputs [1][2]. The portable all-in-one design has a clear purpose that a smartphone alone does not provide without added audio hardware. The cited evidence does not show that using two DACs improves finished-device performance, but the choice is not inherently irrational. The result is a practical and cost-conscious integration of established technologies rather than a measurement-led or innovative design.

## Advice

The FA4 remains purchasable at 146.88 USD and combines local playback, USB DAC operation, bidirectional Bluetooth, native balanced outputs, and a separate line output. The slightly cheaper Shanling M0 Pro bundle does not match all of those connections, format support, or quoted output power. Audio fidelity remains unverified because no controlled FA4 measurements are available, and warranty, firmware, and repair terms should be confirmed with the seller before purchase.
