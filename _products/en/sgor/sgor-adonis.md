## Overview

The SGOR Adonis is a wired hybrid in-ear monitor produced by Dongguan Kangrui Electronic Technology Co., Ltd. and distributed internationally through audio retailers. It combines a 10mm dynamic driver with a 30095 balanced armature unit in a semi-open acoustic housing, and the standard version is priced at 18.00 USD [1][2]. The design inherits the semi-open acoustic cavity from SGOR's earlier Venus model, adding a balanced armature driver for treble extension and a mirror-plated alloy faceplate. A detachable 0.78mm 2-pin cable system with a 3.5mm single-ended termination is included [1].

## Technology Level

$$ \Large \text{0.1} $$

The Adonis uses mature commodity IEM components: a 10mm dynamic driver, a 30095 balanced armature unit, passive crossover, semi-open acoustic shell, 0.78mm 2-pin detachable connector, and OFC silver-plated cable [1][2]. No proprietary or patented technology is documented in the accessible official and retailer material. The all-analog passive architecture involves no digital or software integration and provides no documented barrier to replication. Several marketed acoustic claims, including standing-wave suppression by the mirror-plated faceplate and proximity-effect reduction by the star-twisted OFC cable, are stated without independent verification and cannot be credited as technical achievements. The documented Venus-to-Adonis product progression adds a BA driver for treble extension and reduces impedance from 32Ω to 18Ω, which are functional additions. This is insufficient to offset the mature all-analog component set, limited technical differentiation, and unverified marketing claims.

## Reliability & Support

$$ \Large \text{0.5} $$

The SGOR Adonis carries a 1-year warranty on the IEM units and a 3-month warranty on the cable [5], both below the 2-year industry average. The passive wired design — no battery, no Bluetooth, no active electronics — is inherently low-maintenance, and the detachable 0.78mm 2-pin cable system addresses a common IEM failure point through user-replaceable parts with widely available standard replacements. Support is provided through authorized distributors, primarily Linsoul Audio, rather than a direct global manufacturer support network. No statistical failure rate data is publicly available for this model. SGOR is a recently established brand with limited long-term reliability track record.

## Rationality of Design Philosophy

$$ \Large \text{0.4} $$

The Adonis applies a commoditized 1DD+1BA passive hybrid formula that is widely available from numerous competitors [1][2]. The Venus-to-Adonis product progression demonstrates directional intent: adding a BA driver for treble extension and reducing impedance from 32Ω to 18Ω are user-facing functional improvements. However, several marketed performance claims, including reduced proximity effects from the cable's OFC construction and standing-wave suppression by the mirror-plated alloy faceplate, are presented without independent verification, constituting claims of audible effects for which no scientific evidence is provided. The overall approach follows a conventional recipe with no documented differentiating innovation in acoustic philosophy or execution strategy.

## Advice

The SGOR Adonis at 18 USD is a passive hybrid IEM for which no independent numeric measurement data is currently accessible. Buyers seeking a passive wired IEM should consider that the KZ EDX Pro X at 10.99 USD [3] offers equivalent user-facing functionality and has third-party measurement documentation from Reference Audio Analyzer [4], making it lower-cost for equivalent documented functionality. Buyers with a specific preference for the semi-open acoustic design should note that the manufacturer confirms reduced passive isolation relative to sealed IEMs, which may be relevant in noisier listening environments. Until independent numeric performance measurements for the SGOR Adonis become publicly available, its acoustic characteristics cannot be objectively quantified.
